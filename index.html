Coast FIRE

The "number" is the smallest balance at retirement that still lasts to your plan age with everything above included: taxes, healthcare, Social Security timing, and RMDs. Coast FIRE is the first month your balance, left to grow with no further contributions, would reach that number by your retirement age.

Required minimum distributions

RMDs start at 73 if you were born before 1960, otherwise 75, and use the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. When the RMD is larger than what you need, the app withdraws the full RMD, pays tax on all of it, spends what's needed, and moves the rest into a taxable account that keeps growing and is drawn on if the 401k runs dry.

How the balance gets there

How this works and what it leaves out

Taxes are solved in today's dollars, then the resulting withdrawal is inflated to the retirement year (brackets index with inflation, and Social Security gets cost-of-living adjustments, so this is a fair approximation). In drawdown, withdrawals rise with inflation each year and the remaining balance keeps growing at the rate you set.

Healthcare costs grow at their own inflation rate and are added to spending each year. Medicare IRMAA surcharges use 2026 brackets indexed to inflation and are based on the same year's income (in reality they look back two years). Not modeled: Roth conversions, taxes on growth inside the taxable account, long-term care, sequence-of-returns risk, or any other income. Tax law in your retirement years will differ from 2026. This is a planning estimate, not advice.
